Is This Vegan?

Is it vegan? Know in seconds, and see why.

Take a photo of an ingredient list, a menu or a barcode. The app checks every ingredient and points to the exact words it found on your photo, so you can see the evidence for yourself.

Get it on the App Store

Three clear answers

  • VeganNo animal ingredients found.
  • Not veganThe app shows which ingredients, and where they are on your photo.
  • Worth a checkUnclear ingredients, like natural flavours, are flagged instead of guessed.

What it checks

  • Food labelsHidden animal ingredients like whey, casein, gelatine, carmine (E120), shellac, isinglass and lard.
  • Cosmetics and household productsBeeswax, lanolin, guanine, squalene, carmine and other animal-derived ingredients.
  • Restaurant menusMenu Mode gives every dish its own answer, with what it usually contains, what to ask and how to make it vegan.
  • BarcodesProduct data from Open Food Facts and Open Beauty Facts, including their "may contain" warnings, kept separate from real ingredients.

Made for travel

Staff cards tell restaurant staff what you don't eat, in Thai, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Hindi. They work offline, and your phone can read them aloud. Regional checks know that vegetarian dishes in Thailand often contain fish sauce, and dishes from Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia and Vietnam come with their names in the local script.

Your data

No account and no ads. Your scan history stays on your device. When the check on your device can't decide, the photo and its text are sent securely for AI analysis with Google Gemini, which doesn't use them for training. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Is This Vegan? helps you decide; it's not a certification. Ingredients and recipes change, so check the pack when in doubt, and ask staff when eating out, especially if you have an allergy.

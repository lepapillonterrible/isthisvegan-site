Is This Vegan?

Product data

When you scan a barcode, Is This Vegan? can answer straight away for products we've already checked.

Some of those products were checked from their record on Open Food Facts rather than from a label in a shop. We read the ingredients in the record and decided whether the product is vegan. That list is derived from Open Food Facts, so we share it under the same licence.

  • checked-products.json: products from Vietnam, Japan and South Korea, with their barcode, our verdict, the animal ingredients we found, the date we read the record, and a link to the product on Open Food Facts.

Recipes change, so always check the label in your hand.
